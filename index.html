how it works

  • tap the shutter for one photo
  • hold it for a burst
  • tap, then hold to film: every frame, like a video
  • slide up or down on the picture to zoom
  • ⚡ top left, when your phone allows it: flashlight, or a screen flash on the selfie camera
  • the box by the shutter overlays your last photo, to line up the next
  • tap the pile of photos to start over
  • ✓ when you have two or more: make the GIF, then save it to Photos
  • no camera needed: the photo button bottom left turns photos or a video you already have into a GIF
